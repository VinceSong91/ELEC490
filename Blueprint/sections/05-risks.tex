\section{Risks and Safety Hazards}
Risks are assessed provisionally on likelihood (L) and severity (S) scales from 1 (low) to 5 (high); score = L$\times$S. These are planning estimates, not measured probabilities. A relevant laboratory electrical safety procedure and component datasheets must be cited and reviewed before energizing hardware.
