\section*{Executive Summary}\addcontentsline{toc}{section}{Executive Summary}
Walking golfers currently use separate equipment to transport clubs, track club inventory, and obtain distance information. The ASCC prototype combines these tasks in one motorized push cart. It will follow one golfer at a controlled distance, stop in response to obstacles or a wireless emergency-stop command, detect removal and return of tagged clubs, and show GPS-derived distance to a green on an onboard display.

Development proceeds through early drive and tag-sensing proofs of concept, independent following and safety prototypes, a display/GPS prototype, integrated cart operation, and outdoor verification and validation. The intended operating scenario is a single golfer on open, dry grass, with an operator supervising moving tests. Shot tracking, automated club recommendations, wet-weather operation, and multi-person following are outside the committed scope. The estimated project cost is CAD~595, including contingency, against a CAD~600 budget. The design will be judged using the traceable requirements and acceptance tests defined in this blueprint.\clearpage
