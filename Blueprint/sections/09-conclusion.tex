\CourseSection{Conclusion}\label{sec:conclusion}
\Guidance{Summarize the project and the evidence established in Sections 1--5. End with the expected contribution and the team's readiness to proceed.}

The proposed \ac{ASCC} is a phone-free, autonomous golf pushcart that combines equipment transport with club tracking and distance information. Unlike existing follow carts, which only carry the clubs, the \ac{ASCC} will use RFID-based club identification to keep an inventory of the bag and warn of a club left behind, and \ac{GPS} to show the distance to the green. It will follow the golfer using sensor-based tracking, obstacle detection, and a remote emergency stop. The project will integrate these subsystems into a single working prototype, with testing progressing from controlled subsystem tests to outdoor validation with a golfer and golf bag. Seven milestones will guide development through final system testing by March 2027. An existing Raspberry Pi and ultrasonic sensors will be combined with purchased components and a used motorized golf cart, for an estimated total cost of 595 CAD. The completed prototype is expected to reduce the physical effort of managing golf equipment and to give the golfer club and distance information without a phone or a separate tracking device.
