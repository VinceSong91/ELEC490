\section{Conclusion}
ASCC addresses the gap between following carts and separate golf-information devices by integrating transport, club inventory and green-distance display. The design is feasible only if the team demonstrates safe tracking and stopping before unsupervised-looking operation is attempted. The requirements-to-prototypes-to-tests chain establishes specific exit gates, while the CAD~595 estimate leaves only CAD~5 under the budget ceiling. The main limitations are outdoor tracking reliability, uncertain tag-read geometry, GPS error, and weather/terrain restrictions. Early prototypes should quantify these issues, and the team should narrow the operating envelope or redesign a subsystem if acceptance thresholds are not achieved. Current verified progress is limited to the proposal and the team's reported existing hardware; next steps are to confirm the chassis, finalize safety architecture, source components and run P01/P02 feasibility trials.\par
