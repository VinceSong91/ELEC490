
\CourseSection{Resource Requirements}
\label{sec:resources}

The ASCC has a project budget of CAD \$600. Several key components, including the Raspberry Pi, ultrasonic sensors, and golf push cart, are already owned or borrowed, reducing the overall cost. The remaining budget provides flexibility for unexpected expenses, replacements, or design changes. Additional components, including motors and GPS tracking hardware, may be obtained through the ECE Department or Ingenuity Labs. However, their estimated costs are included in the budget until their availability is confirmed. Table~\ref{tab:resource-budget} summarizes the estimated costs.

\CourseSubsection{Facilities and Contingencies}

Development will require electronics tools and access to indoor and outdoor testing areas. Tools can be found utilizing the '65 ECE lab, and testing will occur in controlled areas and hopefully on a real golf course for the finished prototype. Python and Git will support software development.

The estimated cost is \$480, leaving \$120 below the \$600 budget limit. This remaining budget allows for additional components or design adjustments if needed.
