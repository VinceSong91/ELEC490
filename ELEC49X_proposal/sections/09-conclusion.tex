\CourseSection{Conclusion}\label{sec:conclusion}
\Guidance{Summarize the project and the evidence established in Sections 1--5. End with the expected contribution and the team's readiness to proceed.}

The proposed Autonomous Smart Caddy Cart (ASCC) phone-free, autonomous golf pushcart designed to combine equipment transport with caddy-like assistance. Unlike existing follow carts that primarily address club transportation, the ASCC will use RFID-based club identification, GPS-based club location to prevent lost clubs and personalized club recommendations as additional features. The ASCC will still provide smooth traversing through the golf course utilizing sensor-based golfer tracking, obstacle detection, and emergency stopping. The project will focus on developing and integrating these subsystems into a single working prototype, with testing progressing from controlled subsystem tests to outdoor validation with a golfer and golf bag. Seven milestones will guide development through final system testing by March 2027. Existing Raspberry Pi and ultrasonic sensors will be combined with purchased components and a used motorized golf cart, with an estimated total cost of 595 CAD. The completed prototype is expected to reduce the physical effort of managing golf equipment while providing a more integrated product combining both features of an autonomous pushcart and a human caddy.
